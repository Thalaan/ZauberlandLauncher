# Privacy

Zauberland Launcher is a desktop application that runs entirely on your computer.

- **Sign-in.** You sign in with your own Microsoft account in your web browser. The launcher never sees your password. It receives OAuth tokens from Microsoft, Xbox Live and Minecraft services and uses them only to start the game and join our server.
- **Storage.** Tokens, your player name and UUID are stored only in the launcher's configuration folder on your own device so you do not have to sign in every time. Removing the account in the launcher deletes them.
- **No transmission.** The launcher has no server side. It does not send tokens, names, usage data, crash reports or anything else to the developers or to third parties.
- **Downloads.** The launcher downloads the Minecraft client, NeoForge and mod files from their official sources (Mojang, NeoForged, Modrinth, CurseForge, mod authors' GitHub releases) and verifies them by hash.
- **Contact.** Questions: open an issue in this repository.
